# Phase 1 research protocol

## Working title
Design and Development of an Integrated Planetary Heritage Intelligence Platform for the Documentation, Geospatial Analysis and Scientific Preservation of Human-Made Objects on the Moon and Mars.

## Aim
Develop a reproducible, evidence-led data foundation for a public planetary digital museum and a research-oriented planetary data science laboratory.

## Method
1. Define the 20-object seed inventory.
2. Identify authoritative mission, archive, scientific and image sources.
3. Extract individual factual claims and record their source.
4. Preserve coordinate frames, location types, uncertainty and conflicting values.
5. Standardise records into documented CSV/JSON schemas.
6. Validate uniqueness, required fields, data types and provenance.
7. Grade evidence at claim level and log review decisions.
8. Produce a data-quality report before any statistical or machine-learning claims.

## Research integrity
No missing coordinate is to be inferred or set to zero. Mission landing date and object deployment date must not be conflated. An image is not approved solely because its filename or caption resembles an object name. Distinguish direct observation, official mission description, derived estimate and secondary interpretation.

## Phase 1 scope boundary
Phase 1 is inventory construction, source acquisition planning, schema design and data quality validation. It does not claim to have trained or validated an AI model, completed a planetary GIS analysis, or independently verified all 20 objects.
